# v3 Review: Volatility-weighting vs. v1 (Equal-weight)

## Structural Change Evaluated
This version implements the critic's approved proposal: replace v2's binary volatility eligibility cutoff with inverse realized volatility weighting among eligible names. The eligibility test (momentum > 0, volume ratio > 1.0) and all other logic are unchanged from v1, per instructions.

## Evidence-backed Weakness
Volatility-weighting did not produce improvement in risk-adjusted or absolute returns. All v3 configs underperform v1 and fixed benchmarks (plain_mom, ew_bh) on Sharpe, Sortino, and CAGR—especially on validation. For example, v3's best val_sharpe is 0.55 and val_cagr is 0.102, while v1 achieves val_sharpe 0.53–0.54 and plain_mom / ew_bh both exceed 0.86 val_sharpe and 0.17 val_cagr. Turnover remains high, and no efficiency gain is observed. 

## Overfit Risk/Robustness
There is no evidence of overfit: validation and development results are both flat and unimproved. All tested top_n values (2, 3, 4) yield near-identical, mediocre results.

## Potential Downside/Missed Opportunity
Persistently high turnover (~12) and unchanged selection logic leave open the possibility that alternate constraints (e.g., sector, dynamic N, hybrid weights) could be more beneficial, but were not explored here.

## Summary
Volatility-weighting on top-N momentum picks failed to improve any risk, return, or cost-adjusted metric, and validation underperforms v1 and benchmarks. No evidence supports adopting this revision.
